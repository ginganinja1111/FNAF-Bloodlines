CHARACTERS

Gage

Gage is an 18 year old trying to keep his family afloat through one of the hardest times in their lives. Kind and determined, he refuses to give up when things get difficult. His curiosity often pushes him to ask questions others would rather leave unanswered. Gage believes in doing the right thing, even when the truth comes with a cost.

Mike

Mike is one of the newest night guards at Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria. Calm under pressure and quick to earn people's trust, he becomes Gage's partner during the overnight shift. While the two face the dangers of the pizzeria together, Mike struggles with challenges of his own. Some secrets are easier to hide than others.

Noah

Noah has worked at Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria for years and knows the building better than anyone else. Laid back, sarcastic, and always ready with a joke, he keeps the night shift from feeling too serious. Behind the humor, Noah is a loyal friend who steps up when people need him most. He has seen more than he is willing to admit, and some of his stories sound too strange to be true. As the mystery surrounding the pizzeria grows, Noah finds himself pulled back into a past he thought he had left behind.

William

William Afton is the owner of Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria and takes great pride in his work. Calm, professional, and always in control, he expects the same level of dedication from everyone around him. His attention to detail and commitment to keeping the restaurant running have earned him the respect of both employees and customers. Behind his composed demeanor, William carries burdens few people ever see. Some parts of his life are better left out of the spotlight.
